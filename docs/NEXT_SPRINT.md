# Campaign roadmap — Cold Signal

## Built and playable

- Original Vesper-9 / mothership setting, with the original Mars presentation retained for migrated legacy saves.
- Persistent archive depth leads, planetary core records, core-fuel reserves, and a destination board.
- Mothership loop: core recovery unlocks the jump drive; drive into the landed ship and press E; W takes off and S lands. Orbit-world jumps spend dedicated core fuel, never the miner’s local fuel.
- Six deterministic planetary worlds with separate persistent tunnels and discovery state.
- Visible seeded ore yields (0.5, 1, 2, 3 units), fractional cargo, and proportional selling value.
- Drill-reactive, quantized synthesized music with saved mute and separate music/effects mix levels.
- Recovered route signals and archive hashes each add a distinct four-note hook on the next score beat, routed through the music mix.
- Explored-tunnel map that hides unseen ore, return-fuel estimate, and a conservative reserve indicator.
- Drill upgrades that cut 1/1/2/3/4 blocks by level and visibly enlarge pod artwork alongside cargo upgrades; the compact collision body still fits old tunnels.
- Four optional route signals in authored Cryo Shelf chambers. They record lore and pay a $200 survey grant; they do not assemble or gate the mothership.
- Prism Fault's rare luminous geodes, which stand out visually and guarantee a three-unit ore payout.
- Mars Frontier's thermal seams and Hull Graveyard's ark-alloy caches; both are seed-stable, locally highlighted, and guarantee richer two-unit finds.
- Purchasable mining-charge packs, with a 1.2-second fuse, a 13-tile blast, and physical ore pickups that persist per region.
- Hab 07 paint bay with three credit-earned pod finishes, active paint preview, and save-backed ownership/equipment; paint is visual only.
- Style bay offers four pilot suit palettes, four hull decals, and four silhouette profiles alongside pod finishes; profiles also change cabin glow, and every cosmetic remains visual-only.
- Optional $420 salvage magnet reels physical charge drops toward the pod through open tunnels from 190 px; cargo capacity and solid walls still constrain recovery.
- Expedition briefing lists the movement/sale/map/pause controls and frames the cargo-versus-return-fuel decision before launch.
- New Expeditions open an interactive Vesper system atlas and a brief mothership touchdown / miner deployment animation. The first planetary core is clearly presented as the source of interplanetary fuel.
- Outpost archive records the unknown deep signal as an unresolved lead or recovered transmission with its paid bounty; existing save flags drive the display.
- The explored map marks nearby surveyed ore with a five-ore and signature-find legend; undiscovered blocks remain hidden.
- World crystals and explored-map markers use distinct ore-specific silhouettes, with the same shapes repeated in the map legend so color is not the only ore cue. On the globe, ore clusters now scale uniformly into each curved tile footprint, keeping their crystal shapes intact as cells narrow near the core.
- The outpost archive keeps a per-region survey ledger: deepest scan, recovered Cryo route signals, and each region's optional hash; untouched regions remain clearly unvisited.
- The Pod Workshop lets the player choose a free Seam Cutter, Surveyor, Hauler, or Balanced specialization before a trip. Paths change hard-rock cutting speed, scanner reveal radius, or cargo capacity and can be switched while docked.
- The landed mothership is visible from the surface, with an E boarding hint nearby and an orbit-world board after takeoff.
- Each region contains one optional fictional navigation hash with a unique checksum and archive lore entry. Hashes are offline, have no cash value, and persist in the existing milestone list without a save migration.
- Each recovered regional hash unlocks a distinct named crew voice log. Four recordings connect the regional expeditions to the mothership signal mystery; progress reuses existing hash milestones and adds no save fields.
- Recovering all four crew logs reveals Asha Vale's final message and triggers an archive-complete cue when the last hash is drilled; earlier records show a spoiler-free remaining count.
- Outpost Sell / Service / Upgrades tabs support one continuous visit, preserve the sale receipt, explain upgrade benefits/shortfalls, and keep keyboard focus inside the open dialog after transactions.
- Seeded dirt, basalt, and hard-rock surface details, connected surveyed ore veins, directional side-cut debris, and a distinct hard-rock impact cue add depth without changing saved geology.
- Full and partial cargo-loss warnings account for every ore tile in a multi-block cut and remain visible through the hold-to-cut delay; release cancels before terrain changes.
- The HUD warns at 112 px/s descent speed, before the 280 px/s landing-damage threshold, and tells the player to hold W to brake. A restrained peripheral rush cue now scales with inward fall speed; it clears on braking, respects reduced-motion settings, and is suppressed while paused. The six-seed campaign reaction sweep now completes at 600 ms after this warning with a fuel-tank purchase after the first recovered ship part; at 800 ms at least one seed still loses the pod. Player noticeability and effect on ordinary landings still need human judgment.

## Later ideas

- **Civilized planets and larger Vesper system — design concepts ready.** The art study now shows three populated-world styles, pedestrians walking on elevated city decks, tiny sprite proportions/walk poses, and a single continuous cutaway from the inhabited surface to mineable underground city levels. Future gameplay can connect player-funded population growth to ore demand; gas giants and playable moons belong on the expanded system map. Small co-op remains a possible later direction. No city planet or pedestrian movement is implemented yet. See [the populated-world visual study](concepts/populated-planets-and-citizens-v1.svg) and [civilized planets design](CIVILIZED_PLANETS_DESIGN.md).
- **Colony trade and defense loop — implemented; surface-threat variety and human playtest remain.** Planet-specific ore orders, rotating demand, linked-post premiums, and modular surface walls/gates are playable. Walls can connect edge-to-edge and raiders breach them before attacking optional habitats or pylons; a nearby miner opens gates. Damaged barriers are repairable at the surface yard. System tests cover placement, limits, breaches, and gate behavior. Signals remain optional and do not gate core-fuel travel. See [the surface colony design](SURFACE_COLONY_NETWORK_DESIGN.md).
- **Additional planet environment variety — first visual pass implemented.** Six planet-specific landmark families now add deterministic, world-anchored silhouettes to the curved surface and small markers to the orbital cutaway. This is visual-only and does not change terrain, collision, or mining. Browser review is still needed for readability across worlds, hemispheres, and the wrap seam; biomes such as water or jungle and matching material/gameplay mechanics remain future work. See [the planet surface art guide and concept board](PLANET_SURFACE_ART_GUIDE.md).

After the first chapter demonstrates replay value, consider deeper crew/archive arcs and a richer restored-ship hub. The optional regional hashes now unlock four named crew voice logs. Pilot specializations are implemented at Hab 07. Do not add wallets, tradable tokens, NFTs, or blockchain dependencies.

- **Longer-term — mobile optimization.** Once desktop campaign pacing and controls are settled, adapt the HUD and menus for phone/tablet viewports, add comfortable touch movement/drill/thrust controls, and profile rendering and audio on lower-powered devices. Keep this out of immediate priorities until the core loop and desktop balance are validated.

### Design and feel backlog

- **Drilling animation variety — material impact variety, tiered hardware, resonance rings, active laser beam, and emitter vent implemented.** Dirt throws soft dust, rock chips, hard seams spark, Cryo Shelf ice kicks up frost, ores/geodes shard, and campaign finds glint. The drill extends during a cut and recoils at breakthrough. Auger teeth, resonance nodes, frame supports, and laser heat fins signal higher hardware; tier 3+ breakthroughs add colored rings, while the Laser Miner draws a high-contrast channel to its active target and throws bright sparks on a break. Its heat meter appears while in use, cools on release, and signals the brief vent pause at full heat. Remaining: human inspection at varied zoom/depth and subjective judgment of the beam/vent rhythm.
- **Fall-aware camera and speed feedback — implemented; human feel check open.** The camera eases up to 96 px ahead along gravity beyond the warning threshold and recenters after braking. A peripheral rush cue scales with inward fall speed and clears on braking; it stays out of the playfield, stops while paused, and is disabled by reduced-motion settings. Isolated Chrome verifies cue activation and safe braking. Keep the approaching floor sharp; use human feedback before adding shake, zoom, stretch, wind audio, or a braced fall pose.
- **Winch extraction — curved mined tunnels implemented; browser feel check remains.** The installed Surface Winch now searches for a connected, empty-cell path to the Hab 07 yard on the current crust, displays the cable around tunnel bends, and thrusts along successive waypoints while R is held. Route planning avoids built service beacons, turrets, and platforms, refuses to engage if the surface exit is blocked, and estimates fuel from the visible detour as well as depth. System coverage returns a pod around a mined rock plug, on both crusts, and past a service beacon bypass with no hull damage; it also confirms a closed surface exit disables the route. Earlier isolated Chromium testing verified the straight-shaft return and dock cue; the new curved route has not yet been checked in a browser or by a player.
- **Core transition — full automated route and save/reload verified; human feel check pending.** The miner stays upright while the camera turns through the core crossing, with left/right steering relative to the screen. A disposable Chromium fixture crosses in both directions, docks on both surfaces through the curved town decks, saves/reloads on each hemisphere, and confirms the one-time claim and home-side state persist without overlap. That fixture uses a prepared bore and stasis; ordinary mining through the passage and the transition's timing/readability still need human playtesting before tuning camera effects.
Combat remains deliberately light: a clear-path drill can repel rock swimmers, with built sentry turrets as an optional defense. Broader weapons, extra survival meters, recipe crafting, monetization, and online systems remain outside the game scope.

- **Milestone-based upgrade progression and sixth planet chapter — implemented.** Levels 1–5 are available from the start; the next bands open at level 6 after the first planetary core, level 11 after two planetary core records, and level 16 after the complete six-world core ledger. Progression remains uncapped after the final gate, and older saves need no schema migration. Cinder Vale remains the fifth known world; its five core records chart Vesper-9, a hidden return-world chapter with lantern-grove and living-crystal strata, richer caves and geodes, a unique Lantern Bloom find, and the crew’s Return Bloom core record. The star map previews the chapter lock and its objective; the fifth core update names the newly charted world, and the sixth core unlocks the top upgrade tier. System tests cover the unlock sequence, new core, routes, archive and uncapped upgrades. The browser regression covers the dossier, but browser play was not available in this session. Human pacing and drill-defended return balance remain for later playtesting.

- **Interplanetary miner opening and drill response — implemented; browser feel check pending.** New expeditions start on a compact 480 m-deep Cryo Shelf, while later unvisited worlds remain 1,800 m and existing saves keep their own geometry. The recommended starting planet is called out in the interactive system atlas. Pointer movement steers the searchlight and drill aim; holding the left button cuts along the ray, and the aim direction remains active through open tunnels for rock-swimmer hits. A/D steering into a solid obstruction now offers that block to the drill automatically, and a charted-globe regression confirms the miner can cut through and continue safely. Mouse targeting can also clear a tile under the miner while protecting its other overlap cells. Escape-suit and emergency recovery land near the current longitude and preserve the current hemisphere instead of snapping to the original landing pad; emergency recovery still forfeits carried ore. Curved-chart collision holds the miner at its last clear position when a polar cell blocks movement. Surface raids start later to reduce interruptions. Running out of fuel underground leaves the miner and mine in place, pauses the expedition, and offers a beacon refuel or explicit cargo-forfeiting recovery. Browser playtesting is still needed for aim readability, hit feedback, and rescue timing.
- **Interplanetary colony network — globe construction, local services, defenses, trade contracts, warehouses, walls, gates, recoverable sales, and buyer choice implemented.** Press B on exposed charted crust to place persistent colony buildings; placement follows the globe and checks cost, limits, station clearance, and footprint. Ore storage is per planet. At a Trading Post with multiple linked colonies, choose a local or remote buyer; each buyer's rotating demand changes the sale bonus, while local delivery orders pay only when selling to the current planet. Receipts preserve the chosen buyer and exact payout, and a failed save restores cargo, credits, and market progress. The Vesper Relay adds a linked-post premium; the Cargo Tug consumes stored ore at Cryo Shelf, Hull Graveyard, and Vesper-9 to unlock remote withdrawals from built colony warehouses. Raiders telegraph attacks, can be driven off by drills or pylons, and damage optional habitats, pylons, and barriers; nearby gates lift for the miner. Hab 07 repairs damaged structures. System coverage includes seams, interaction range, pylon interception, barrier placement/limits/breaches, gate behavior, save migration, contracts, warehouse transfer, and trade routes. The Shard Manta adds a telegraphed cave charge enemy, and the Hull Graveyard-only Hull Scrapper deflects frontal hits and rewards flanking. Both can be driven off with the drill. Remaining: browser readability and balance review of these encounters, human campaign/balance playtesting. Keep mobile optimization in Later ideas as previously requested.
