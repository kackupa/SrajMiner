## Player checks and tuning

Use the [15-minute playtest sheet](PLAYTEST.md) for a few normal trips, then a longer campaign session. Record observations before changing economy, damage, or camera settings.

- [ ] **Round planets.** Judge zoom speed and readability, surface/underground cues, ordinary mining through the core, camera-turn timing, screen-relative steering, far-side navigation, and near-core cut yield versus fuel cost. Prepared-bore fixtures already cover crossings and returns but do not prove ordinary mining feels right.
- [ ] **Opening and drilling.** Check the compact 480 m Cryo Shelf, mouse aim during flight, side-obstruction drilling, hit feedback, and the reported blocked-movement symptom. Check low-fuel rescue timing without automatic recovery. Existing saves must retain their original geometry.
- [ ] **Drill tiers.** Check adjacent-tile starter reach, extended reach and swath, material debris, resonance rings, laser visibility, heat/vent rhythm, ore pickup distance, and a return trip at each tier. Inspect varied depths and zoom levels.
- [ ] **Falls and rescue.** Judge warning noticeability, camera lookahead, braking time, optional grapple timing and tether readability, curved-route winch use, and escape-suit flight. Check reduced-motion behavior. Safe ledges and open-shaft falls must not trigger the grapple.
- [ ] **Mothership.** Judge boarding range, destination selection and route preview, takeoff/landing animation, fuel deductions, and return routes. WASD remains a mining control, not a star-map control. Include far-hemisphere launches.
- [ ] **Colony use.** Check curved town navigation, service locations, stackable wall placement, gates, repairs, local warehouses, remote buyers, contracts, demand, and the Vesper Relay/Cargo Tug projects. Judge whether the Survey Array's two-tile bonus is useful without replacing scanner upgrades.
- [ ] **Threats.** Check Rock Swimmer warnings, Shard Manta charge readability, Hull Scrapper flanking, surface raids, drill hit cues, and defensive structures during ordinary mining. Tune arrival rate and damage from observed encounters.
- [ ] **Economy and rewards.** Record trip length, haul, sale value, service cost, return fuel, hull damage, core progress, dead ends, and post-level-5 purchases. Check signature-find rarity/value, charge fuse timing, magnet price/range, and cosmetic prices.
- [ ] **Accessibility and sound.** Judge ore silhouettes without color, contrast on varied displays, small suit/decal details, and music/effects balance by listening. Existing Chromium and Edge checks cover selected desktop layouts; they do not replace player feedback.
